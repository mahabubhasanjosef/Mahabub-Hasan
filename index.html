01 / ABOUT ME

Professional Profile

আমার পরিচিতি

I am Mahabub Hasan, a television journalist working as a Crime Investigative Reporter at Jagoroni TV since March 2022. I completed my Honours degree in Management from National University in 2018. My work and interests include field reporting, crime and public-interest stories, communication, writing and digital content creation.
